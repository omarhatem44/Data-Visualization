Data visualization portfolio

Four of its twelve charts, live in your browser: hover, zoom, filter and press play. Static versions and the code for all twelve are in the repository.

Animated bubble chart

Every country as a bubble: income on a log scale, life expectancy up the side and population as size, from 1952 to 2007.

Press play or drag the slider. Hover a bubble for its numbers, and click a continent in the legend to hide it.

plotly express, animation frames, log axis, shared template

Choropleth map

Life expectancy in 2007, country by country, from orange for the shortest lives to blue for the longest.

Hover a country for its value. Drag to pan and scroll to zoom.

Treemap

Population by continent and country. Area shows how many people live there; colour shows life expectancy on the same scale as the map.

Click a continent to zoom in, then the top bar to zoom back out.

plotly express, population-weighted colour, uniform label sizes

Parallel categories

Titanic passengers traced from ticket class through age and sex group to the outcome. Unlike a Sankey diagram, every group keeps its whole path.

Hover a ribbon for counts and shares, and drag a category to reorder it.

plotly graph objects, parcats trace, fixed category order
